The platform

One intelligence layer. Three things it has to do.

The same three capabilities, whether it is watching the line or working on it.

Perceive

It sees what is actually happening — parts, people, tools, state — and names it.

Objects, actions and conditions, not motion blobs
Plant cameras, or the robot’s own sensors
Continuous, not sampled

Reason

Your standard, written in plain English, becomes the rule it judges against.

Plain English in, no rule engine to maintain
Knows a step skipped from a step out of order
Learns which exceptions are worth your attention

Act

One decision, whatever carries it out.

An alert, a flag on the traveler, a hold on the lot
Or a physical move, when it is holding the part
Same judgment either way
